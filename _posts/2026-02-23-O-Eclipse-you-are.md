title: "O Eclipse You Are"
date: 2026-02-23

Your gaze, those lucid eyes that mesmerise  
Like moonlight, pristinely serene and calm.  
Your hair, soft riots of ringlets falling in guise  
Of lunar crescents, silvered into balm.  

Your wrath, the supernova of fierce insist  
Like sunlight, scorching through the thickest ice.  
Your stance, to me an immovable schist.  
Of solar will that holds through changing skies.  

Eternal tranquility be it with you, a gentle hush that steadies storms.  
So fragile, tender and luminously kind.  
Yet piercing and raging heat  
Summoning the better man in me from the warmth of your fire.  

The moon of the dark and the sun of the day, dear!  
Would they ever unite?  
Oh they do - O Eclipse you are,  
An ethereal rite.  

But the tribes fearing the omen of your union; bar their doors,  
They hide from the sky's rare crossing; demon devouring the sun.  
How I would go out in the open to see you each day!  
A strange and singular fate.  

For the sun that caresses the fruits,  
For the moon that sends shivering winds,  
The eclipse is beautiful today,  
Isn't it?
