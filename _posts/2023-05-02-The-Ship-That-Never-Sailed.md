title: "The Ship That Never Sailed"
date: 2026-05-02

She longed for the ship on a stormy night  
That might take her to the island she wished.  
For the lonely port, do the ships come light?  
But if the dream be true, so the feeling be rich.  
Anchored against the rocky spray,  
Belief took over the sky, all dark and gray.  

Lightnings crackling and thunders rumbling,  
High waves before low grounds standing.  
As she waited - oh! her faith untrue,  
The sprinkle turned volley, continued through.  
Completely soaked, yet she stayed nonchalant,  
Peering through the horizon, testing her daunt.  
Only flying mists were seen,  
Not of a faith yielding scene.  

Coughing frustrations in the cold,  
One last look for the old,  
Same frosty ambit of nothing’s nest.  
Before chasing the absolute loneliness,  
Grave of efforts; dug and dusted.  
The dock and she; two river borders, twisted.  
Another step back turned oblivion.  
She left and searched beyond.  

My! God of the clearing clouds, venturing back, the ship  
Lay immovable, its toll taken by the rocks.  
If the ignorant glance were timely late,  
Would she have known the ship returned for her?  
Or would luck have led her steps back to the place  
As though they were meant to meet?  

The ship never sailed.  
The ship never sailed.
